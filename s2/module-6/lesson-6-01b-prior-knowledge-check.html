In previous grades, you learned about keywords that represent mathematical operations and how to express mathematical descriptions as a numerical expression. A numerical expression is an expression that uses numbers and operations to mathematically represent a verbal description. Numerical expressions can be evaluated using the order of operations to find the total.


Understanding Mathematical Descriptions

Mathematical descriptions use words to describe operations and relationships between numbers. Our goal is to turn these words into a numerical expression that we can calculate.

An example of a mathematical description would be "four less than five times the sum of three and seven."

Identify the Operations:

  • "Sum of 3 and 7" suggests an addition operation.
  • "5 times" indicates multiplication.
  • "4 less than" suggests subtraction.

Conclusion

Creating numerical expressions from mathematical descriptions is like solving a puzzle. By identifying the operations and translating them step by step, you can turn words into mathematical expressions. This skill is not only essential for your math class but also useful in real-life situations.
