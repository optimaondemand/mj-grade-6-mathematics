Conclusion

Evaluating algebraic expressions is like solving a puzzle. By substituting values for variables and following the order of operations, you can find out what these expressions represent in real numbers. This skill is not only useful in math class but also in solving everyday problems. When you evaluate the operations in the expression, you need to remember your rules for adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing integers. You also need to remember the order of the operations and when they work together from left to right.
