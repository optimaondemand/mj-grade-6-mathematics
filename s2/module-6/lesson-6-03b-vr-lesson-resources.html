🎮 ACTIVITY

Enter the virtual bank location and use sticky notes to write out some real-world context that could be interpreted into algebraic inequalities like "You have less than $55 in your account" or "You need at least $35 to open an account". Then use the IFXs number line, open circles, closed circles, and 3D pen to represent thes descriptions as inequalities such as .

Create 2-4 different situations, represent them as inequalities using the IFXs. Then place the numbers on the number line and use open circles for inequalities that do not include the number and closed circles for those that do. Draw arrows to show the range of possible values for x.

Once you have represented several inequalities, take a snapshot of your work or record a MyRec explaining your representations. This will help you reflect on what you've learned and show your understanding of inequalities.
