In previous grades, you learned how to represent a real-world context or word problem with a numerical expression using keywords and phrases. As we get ready to explore writing algebraic expressions, it is important to review these keywords and phrases that help create numerical expressions. A numerical expression is a mathematical phrase that can include numbers, operations (like addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division), and sometimes variables.


Understanding Real-World Contexts

Real-world contexts are situations or problems from everyday life that can be described using mathematics. For example, if you're saving money, calculating distances, or even baking, you're dealing with real-world contexts that can be expressed numerically.


Steps to Write Numerical Expressions

  1. Identify the Numbers: First, find the numbers involved in the situation. For example, if you have 5 apples and buy 3 more, your numbers are 5 and 3.

  2. Determine the Operation: Decide which mathematical operation (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division) best represents the situation. In our apple example, you are adding apples, so the operation is addition.
