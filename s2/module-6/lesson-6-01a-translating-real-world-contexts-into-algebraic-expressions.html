Steps to Write Algebraic Expressions

  1. Identify the Variable: Determine what the variable represents in the real-world situation. It could be anything from the number of items to the distance traveled.

  2. Determine the Operations: Decide which mathematical operations best represent the situation. You might need to add, subtract, multiply, or divide.

  3. Write the Expression: Combine the variable and operations into an algebraic expression.
