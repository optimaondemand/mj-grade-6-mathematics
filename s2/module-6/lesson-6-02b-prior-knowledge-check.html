We have been learning a little bit about parts of an algebraic expression with words like coefficient, terms, like terms, and variables. We are going to do a refresher on some of these vocabulary words, what they mean, and how to identify them in a given algebraic expression. It is important to understand the parts of an algebraic expression as we further explore using algebraic expressions in mathematical and real-world problems.


What is a Linear Algebraic Expression?

A linear algebraic expression is a mathematical phrase that can include numbers, variables (symbols that represent unknown values), and operation symbols (like + or -). It's called 'linear' because each term is either a constant or a variable raised to the first power.


Coefficients

  • Definition: A coefficient is a number used to multiply a variable. It tells you how many times to use the variable in a multiplication.
