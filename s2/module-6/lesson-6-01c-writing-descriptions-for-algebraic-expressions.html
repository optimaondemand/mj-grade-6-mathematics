Example 1

Choose the mathematical description that could be represented by the expression .

  1. 10 less than a number
  2. the quotient of 10 and a number
  3. a number subtracted from 10
  4. a number decreased by 10

We want to begin by looking at the statements in the options to determine which ones could represent subtraction.

Quotient means division. So, we can begin by eliminating option B since this statement represents division.

Next, we need to look at the remaining options to determine if 10 is being subtracted from w or if w is being subtracted from 10. Order is important when working with subtraction.

Looking at Option A the phrase "less than" reverses the order of the number and variable. So, instead of being 10-w, it would be w-10. This does not match the given algebraic expression.

If we look at Option C, this represents 10-w since we want a number subtracted from 10. Since we are subtracting from 10, it reverses the order as well. This expression matches the given expression.

Last, if we look at Option D, a number decreased by 10 is represented by the expression w-10. We need to decrease (subtract) a number by 10. This does not match the given expression.

Therefore, the correct answer is Option C.
