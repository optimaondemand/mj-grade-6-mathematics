🎮 ACTIVITY

Using a set of data from previous activities, like the median incomes of families that might live in the houses on the streets of the Historical city, create two sets of data. One set of data shows an extreme minimum where maybe the family is a one-income house with a below-average salary. Then a second set of data where a family that is a two-income house with above average salaries moved in.

Using the 3D pen or a whiteboard, calculate the mean, median, and range of the original data without the extreme values. Then calculate the measures for the 2 new data sets, one with the extreme minimum and one with the extreme maximum.

Make a myrec discussing the effects of the extreme values when added to the original data set on the statistical measures. What did the below-average salary do to the measures of center and what does it mean for the central tendency? What did it do to the range and what does that mean for the data set? What did the above-average salaries do to the measures?
