📖 PRIOR KNOWLEDGE

In previous grades, you learned how to find the mean and median of a simple set of data. As we get ready to explore finding and interpreting mean and median, it is important to review how we calculate these measures for some small basic sets of data. Let's begin by reviewing what mean and median are.


What is the Mean?

The mean is the average of a set of numbers. It's calculated by adding up all the numbers and then dividing by the number of numbers.

How to Calculate the Mean:

  1. Add up all the numbers in your data set.
  2. Divide the total by the number of numbers in the set.


What is the Median?

The median is the middle value in a set of numbers when they are arranged in order. If there's an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle numbers.

How to Calculate the Median:

  1. Arrange the numbers in numerical order.
  2. Find the middle number. If there are two middle numbers, find their average.
