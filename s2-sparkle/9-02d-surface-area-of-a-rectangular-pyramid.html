In today’s lesson, we will explore what a net of a rectangular pyramid is and how we can use it to find the surface area of the pyramid. A pyramid is a three-dimensional shape that has a base and triangular faces that come together at a single point, called the apex. The net of a rectangular pyramid is a two-dimensional shape that, when folded, forms the pyramid. By the end of this lesson, you’ll understand how to use the net of a rectangular pyramid to calculate its surface area.

What Is a Net?

A net is the flat, unfolded version of a three-dimensional shape. For a rectangular pyramid, the net shows the rectangular base and the four triangular faces laid out flat. Each triangle is connected to one side of the rectangle, and when you fold the net along the edges, it forms the three-dimensional pyramid.

In the images provided, you can see the net of a rectangular pyramid. Notice how the base of the pyramid is a rectangle, and the triangles are connected to the rectangle’s sides. Each triangle represents one of the slanted sides of the pyramid.

Finding the Surface Area Using the Net

The surface area of a rectangular pyramid is the total area of all the faces. To find the surface area, we need to calculate the area of the rectangular base and the areas of the four triangles that make up the slanted sides, and then add them all together. Here’s how to do it:

Step 1: Find the area of the rectangular base

  • The base of the pyramid is a rectangle. To find its area, use the formula Area of Rectangle = base · height.
  • In the first image, the base has a length of 6 units and a width of 4 units, so Area of base = 6 × 4 = 24 square units

Step 2: Find the area of the triangular faces

  • Each triangular face has a base (one side of the rectangle) and a slant height (the height of the triangle). The formula to find the area of a triangle is Area of triangle = 12 · base · height.
  • In the first image, we see the triangle on the side has a base of 6 and a height of 5. So, the area of the triangle would be Area of triangle 1 = 12(6)(5) = 12(30) = 15 squared units.
  • We also notice that there are 2 of these triangles for the face across from it. So, we can multiply that area by 2 and find the area of the front and back faces is 30 squared units.
  • For the left and right triangles, we notice that the base is 4 and the height is 5.7. So, the area of that triangle is Area of triangle 2 = 12(4)(5.7) = (2)(5.7) = 11.4 squared units.
  • Again, there are 2 of these triangles. So, we will multiply by 2 to find the area of both is 22.8 squared units.

Step 3: Add the areas together

  • Once you have calculated the area of the base and the area of each triangular face, add them all together to get the total surface area of the pyramid.

For example:

  1. The area of the rectangular base is 24 square units.
  2. The area of each triangular face is
    • Front and Back = 30 squared units
    • Left and Right = 22.8 squared units
  3. Now, we add together all the areas to find the total surface area.
    • Surface Area = 24 + 30 + 22.8 = 76.8 squared units

Conclusion

By using the net of a rectangular pyramid, we can easily find its surface area. The net allows us to break the pyramid down into its individual faces—a rectangle and four triangles. By calculating the area of each part and adding them together, we can find the total surface area of the pyramid. This method of using a net helps us visualize the shape and understand how to calculate the surface area step by step. Keep practicing, and you’ll be able to find the surface area of any pyramid with ease!
